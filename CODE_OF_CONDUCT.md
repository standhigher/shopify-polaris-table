# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, appearance, disability, nationality, religion, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and act in the project's best interests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information, or other disruptive conduct is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a [private GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/standhigher/shopify-polaris-table/security/advisories/new). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
